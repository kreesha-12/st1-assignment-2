Stakeholder 1: Receptionist- Needs to quickly book, view, edit, and cancel appointments, and search for patient records without duplication or confusion

Stakeholder 2: Practitioner- Needs visibility of their own daily/weekly schedule and appointment history for each patient they see

Stakeholder 3: Clinic Manager- Needs reliable operational reports (e.g. appointment volumes, cancellations, practitioner utilisation) to manage staffing and clinic performance

Stakeholder 4: Patient- Needs confidence that their appointment is correctly booked, not duplicated, and that their personal and medical information is accurate and secure
