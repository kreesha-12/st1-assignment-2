SmartCare Community Clinic currently relies on spreadsheets and paper records to manage patients, practitioners, and appointments. This had led to operational issues including duplicate bookings, difficulty locating patient records and inconsistent appointment status. The clients request is to build a software to help manage these issues, however this is quite broad and lacks details on required features. Before development can begin, this request needs to be broken down into specific, testable requirements covering what data must be stored, what operations users need to perform, and what constraints the system must enforce.
